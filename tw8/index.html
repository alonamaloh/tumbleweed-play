Rules. Side-8 board, CodeCup rules; the two starting stacks may go anywhere. Place a stack on any hex seen by your stacks along straight lines. Its height = number of your stacks that see it; you need at least as many as the opponent sees there, and more than the current stack's height. Replacing works on enemy, neutral, and your own stacks. When territory is settled, the player with more territory wins. Badges show legal moves, including captures and reinforcements on occupied cells.
